# Design review checklist

- Can the critical comparison distinguish the competing accounts?
- Who can be generalized to, and what selection or attrition could distort that inference?
- What is randomized, blocked, matched, counterbalanced, or merely observed?
- What learning, fatigue, carryover, interference, or order effects can arise?
- Is the manipulation separable from demand, salience, difficulty, time, or exposure?
- Can the apparatus and outcomes resolve the construct at the needed scale?
- Are recruitment, burden, hardware, staffing, accessibility, and failure recovery realistic?
- Are the primary estimands and dependence structure identifiable from the design?
- Which hypotheses, outcomes, exclusions, stopping rules, and deviations will be preregistered or labeled exploratory?
