# Learn Style

Turn the researcher's revisions and approved samples into durable style rules and exemplars. Run interactively. Propose durable changes; wait for the researcher's approval before writing any rule or exemplar.

## 1. Establish the comparison

Choose the email or academic track. Obtain `[Original Agent Draft]` and `[Approved Version]`. If the researcher provides only an approved sample, compare it with the closest rule and exemplar rather than inventing an original.

## 2. Analyze the delta

Separate meaning from form. Identify structural cuts, pruned pleasantries, phrasing substitutions, technical specificity, delegation language, hedges, cadence, signatures, and audience modulation. Do not generalize typos, factual corrections, or one-off accommodations into voice rules.

## 3. Extract candidate rules

Generalize the delta into **one or two** actionable, non-redundant guidelines. Each must apply beyond the sample, preserve relevant audience/genre exceptions, and cite a short before/after fragment as evidence. If the current rule already covers the lesson, propose an exemplar-only addition or no change.

## 4. Ask for approval

Present the observed delta, exact proposed rule text, proposed exemplar path, and any privacy or provenance concern. Ask the researcher to approve, revise, or reject. **Silence is not approval.**

## 5. Apply the approved update

After approval only:

- edit your own style rule (for example `.agents/rules/writing-style-email.md` or `.agents/rules/writing-style-academic.md`);
- save the pair under `resources/style-exemplars/emails/` or `academic/` using the next two-digit prefix;
- record `type: style-exemplar`, track, approval date, and source/provenance;
- include `## Original Agent Draft`, `## Approved Version`, and `## Learned Rule` when privacy permits;
- otherwise store a minimal diff or structural summary and link the canonical source.

Never alter the original sent or approved message merely to make it conform. Explain historical exceptions in the exemplar.

## 6. Verify and close out

Re-read for redundancy, faithful meaning, privacy, and conflict with recipient modulation or evidence discipline. Validate this skill when it changes. Use targeted staging only; never push without explicit authorization. Append the required walkthrough to the active handoff.

## Boundaries

- Daily notes and literature notes remain read-only.
- `status: sent` or `ready-to-send` does not itself authorize a new learning update.
- Do not copy raw literature, full email threads, credentials, sensitive personal data, or protected instrument text into the bank.
- Keep style learning separate from factual or policy correction.
