# YouTube Titles: Meetings, Calendars & Email Subject Lines

Transform mundane academic, research, and technical calendar invites and email subject lines into hilarious, hyper-accurate YouTube video titles and clickbait hooks. This skill diagnoses the *real* unspoken stakes, interpersonal tensions, and technical friction of a meeting or email, then packages them into classic YouTube algorithmic tropes.

---

## The Core Surfaces

### Surface A: Meeting & Calendar Invites
- **Where it lives:** Google Calendar, Outlook, scheduling polls, meeting prep notes.
- **The Format:** A punchy video title that turns an ordinary sync into an epic algorithmic spectacle.
- **Bonus Feature:** Include an optional "Meeting Description / Pinned Comment" formatted like a YouTube video description with timestamps (e.g., `0:00 - Small talk, 4:15 - False optimism, 18:30 - Confronting the bug, 29:00 - Scheduling another meeting`).

### Surface B: Email Subject Lines & Preview Hooks
- **Where it lives:** Gmail, Outlook, lab chat announcements.
- **The Format:** A clickbait subject line paired with a matching "inbox preview snippet" (the first 1–2 sentences of the body that appear in the notification banner).
- **Bonus Feature:** "Sponsored Segue" or creator sign-off adapted for academic reality.

---

## Archetype Taxonomy & Formulas

### 1. The High-Stakes Challenge (MrBeast / Beta Squad)
- **Meeting Formula:** `Can [Person A] and [Person B] agree on [Topic] in [N] minutes? (INTENSE)`
- **Email Formula:** `I gave [Team] [Timeframe] to solve [Problem]. Here is what happened.`
- **Hooks:** `(INTENSE)`, `(EXTREME)`, `(GONE WRONG)`, `($10,000 CHALLENGE)`, `[IMPOSSIBLE DIFFICULTY]`.
- **Vibe:** Racing against the clock, impossible constraints, high-energy panic.

### 2. The Investigative Video Essay / Documentary (Johnny Harris / SunnyV2 / Fern)
- **Meeting Formula:** `The Tragic Downfall of [Topic]: How [Catastrophe] Happened [Documentary]`
- **Email Formula:** `The [Topic] Scandal: Why Nobody Talks About [Specific Problem]`
- **Hooks:** `(40:00 Deep Dive)`, `[Documentary]`, `A Cautionary Tale`, `The Truth About [X]`.
- **Vibe:** Somber, analytical melodrama, systemic collapse, forensic post-mortems.

### 3. The YouTuber Apology / "We Need to Talk" (Grey Hoodie on the Floor)
- **Meeting Formula:** `Addressing the [Mistake/Delay] rumors... (serious)`
- **Email Formula:** `We need to talk about [Metric/Feature/Deadline]... (I'm sorry)`
- **Hooks:** `(serious)`, `(not clickbait)`, `(emotional)`, `My Final Statement`, `(addressed to [Name])`.
- **Vibe:** Low production value, defensive justification, awkward transparency, heavy sighs.

### 4. The Sponsored Integration / Sudden Pivot
- **Email Formula:** `We need to discuss [Core Problem] (and a quick word from our sponsor)`
- **Snippet Hook:** *"Before we address why the build crashed at 2:00 AM, let me tell you about our reference manager / Raid Shadow Legends..."*
- **Vibe:** Seamless, jarring transition from critical lab failure into corporate sponsorship.

### 5. The Auto-Generated Topic Track / Music Mix
- **Meeting Formula:** `Mix - [Month/Team] Updates [Official Audio]`
- **Email Formula:** `[Person A] ft. [Person B] - [Pain Point] [Official Visualizer]`
- **Hooks:** `[Official Audio]`, `[Official Music Video]`, `(Acoustic Version)`, `(Continuous DJ Mix)`.
- **Vibe:** Monotonous status updates disguised as certified algorithmic radio bangers.

### 6. The Chaos Livestream / Speedrun / Gaming Meta
- **Meeting Formula:** `[Topic] ANY% Glitchless Speedrun (World Record Attempt)`
- **Email Formula:** `Debugging [Bug/System] LIVE until I lose my mind [Day 4]`
- **Hooks:** `[LIVE]`, `[ANY% GLITCHLESS]`, `(WORLD RECORD)`, `[NIGHTMARE DIFFICULTY]`.
- **Vibe:** Bug fixing, live coding, endurance tests, edge-case exploitation, RNG despair.

### 7. The Unhinged Marketing Hack / Lifehack Clickbait
- **Email Formula:** `[Authority/Advisor] HATES this one weird trick to [Overcome Obstacle]`
- **Hooks:** `The new RA won't BELIEVE what happened`, `DO NOT DO THIS`, `Reviewer 2 DESTROYED by facts and logic`.
- **Vibe:** Classic 2014 tabloid internet clickbait applied to experimental rigor.

### 8. The 24/7 Lo-Fi Chill Stream / Study Beats
- **Meeting Formula:** `lofi hip hop radio - beats to [suffer through task] to [24/7 LIVE STREAM]`
- **Hooks:** `[24/7 LIVE]`, `[chill study beats]`, `relaxing sounds for academic dread`.
- **Vibe:** Monotonous administrative grind, data entry, reference formatting, infinite backlog.

## Recipient & Audience Calibration

Calibrate the level of chaos based on who receives the invite or email:

| Audience | Permitted Chaos | Calibrated Tone & Tropes |
|---|---|---|
| **Student research assistants** | **High / Unhinged** | MrBeast challenges, "I gave the RAs 24 hours", gaming speedruns, playful reference-manager roasts. Keep it fun, encouraging, and communal. |
| **Technical peers** | **Maximum Chaos** | Linus Tech Tips teardowns, livestream coding despair, unhinged bug exposés, build-pipeline existential dread. |
| **Advisors & PIs** | **Clever Satire / Calibrated Irony** | Highbrow intellectual drama, "Addressing the random slope rumors", "The tragic downfall of Table S1", "Can I convince the advisor in 30 minutes". Respectful, self-aware humor. |
| **Facility staff & administrators** | **Mild Situational Humor** | Official music audio, lo-fi study beats, room reservation sagas. Avoid triggering genuine administrative alarm. |
| **Deans, external parties, formal committees** | **Strict Boundary** | **DO NOT USE.** Keep formal and professional. |

## Workflow

1. **Intake Context:**
   - **Target Surface:** Meeting title, calendar event, email subject line, or both?
   - **Parties Involved:** Who is attending or receiving? (PI, collaborator, peer, RA team).
   - **The Declared Agenda:** What is the nominal subject?
   - **The Unspoken Friction / Elephant in the Room:** What is the real stress point? (e.g., *“the headset makes people sick,” “nobody filled out the poll,” “the model won't converge,” “we have 15 minutes left before the deadline”*).

2. **Select 4–6 Diverse Archetypes:**
   - Always provide options across contrasting genres (e.g., 1 Challenge, 1 Video Essay, 1 Apology/Exposé, 1 Official Audio/Mix, 1 Sponsored Pivot / Speedrun).
   - Inject authentic domain terminology: hardware models (`the headset`), software (`the game engine`), statistics (`random slopes`, `p < .05`), or administrative lore (`the IRB portal`, `the scheduling poll`).

3. **Format the Output:**
   - Clear genre tag for each title.
   - For email subjects, include the **Preview Snippet** (the opening hook sentence).
   - For meeting titles, include an optional **Pinned Comment / Description** if requested.

---

## Boundaries

- Never apply unhinged clickbait titles to formal, external communications (e.g. a dean's office, IRB formal notifications, grant officers) unless the researcher explicitly instructs to do so.
- Staged email drafts must still conform to your email safety standards (never auto-send; preserve the researcher's manual review before sending).
