# Deck templates

The detail behind `presentations` § 1. Section numbers below refer to `SKILL.md`.

## The eight templates

All live in `operations/templates/`.

- `Touying Presentation - Research Talk.typ` — one assertion and one primary item of evidence per slide.
- `Touying Presentation - Journal Club.typ` — one-paper background, question, methods, results, interpretation, and critique.
- `Touying Presentation - Synthesis.typ` — a multi-paper lineage that lands on a gap, proposal, or critical test.
- `Touying Presentation - Conference Talk.typ` — 12–15 minutes of results: the full hypothesis spine, weighted toward figures, closing on one contribution.
- `Touying Presentation - Lightning Talk.typ` — 5 minutes, 4–6 slides: one hypothesis, one key result, a take-home with contact.
- `Touying Presentation - Prelim Talk.typ` — prelim or committee: a framework, an aims overview, a hypothesis map per aim, a timeline, and committee questions.
- `Touying Presentation - Lab Update.typ` — a 5–10-minute lab check-in: status, one result or demo, the next milestone, and asks. No spine.
- `Touying Presentation - Lab Meeting.typ` — a full lab-meeting slot on one project: recap of the last public talk, what changed since, open problems put to the room, and next steps.

## Lab-internal talks

Lab Update and Lab Meeting decks are for the lab itself. External talks keep every rule in `SKILL.md`.

- No acknowledgements slide. § 2b's fixed opening starts at motivation.
- The title may be casual, in the presenter's own words (§ 3c).
- Lighter spine. The hypothesis map appears once, usually in the recap. It returns only where a section directly tests a hypothesis. The footer tags each slide's part instead: `section-tag([Recap · Seminar, 16 Sep])` or `section-tag([New since 16 Sep])`. Each part opens with a section divider.
- An open-problem or discussion slide may use an honest question as its headline. Every other slide keeps a declarative headline (§ 3).
